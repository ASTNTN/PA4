BPM Tuning Spaces

Tuning parameters and exploration space for each variant. The user may explore additional parameters too, by changing shared/harness/args.c.

00-GSQ

No tuning parameters. Reference baseline.

01-OPQ

No sequential tuning (loop order fixed). Parallel: schedule, chunk size, reduction — guided by investigation. collapse is illegal; the nowait diagnostic run lands under results/diagnostic/ (--allow-gate-failure).

02-EXQ

Same exploration space as OPQ. Question: do different loop orders require different scheduling strategies?

03-BKQ

Block size (BS) — explore a range, including non-power-of-two values. Also explore schedule/chunk/collapse/nowait as needed.

04-DCQ

LEAF_SIZE (degree threshold) and DEPTH (task-spawn cutoff). Leaf kernel: best of OPQ/EXQ/BKQ, decided by measurement, not assumption.

05-DCP

Race-handling strategy needs discussion first: limited parallelism, private copies, synchronization, task reductions, or custom. May require shared-infrastructure changes (memory allocation, function signatures) — Claude helps implement directly once a strategy is chosen.
